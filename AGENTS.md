# Project Rules and Editorial Standards

## Feed Card Content Guidelines
- **Headline / Title**: Maximum **80 characters** for crisp, high-impact readability.
- **Standard Story Summary**: Set to **190 characters (Malayalam)** (approx. 25–28 words) to perfectly fit the mobile card format without ellipsis truncation.
- **Detailed Reading**: Full article content is revealed when users tap on the card headline or the detailed arrow button.
- **Typography**: English UI elements and labels use Product Sans / Google Sans. Malayalam text uses Noto Sans Malayalam.
- **Movie Review Section**: Show Title, Synopsis, and a dedicated arrow button to open the detailed page with all movie sections (Cast, Crew, Specs, Highlights, Audience Reviews) without any IMDb rating elements.
- **Monetization & Partner Stories**: Sponsored articles link directly to partner URLs on headline touch without disruptive CTA buttons.
